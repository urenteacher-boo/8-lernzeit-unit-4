📖 Read the Text
The Story of Solomon Northup
A Secret Diary

This is a real story. Solomon Northup was a free Black man who was kidnapped and forced into slavery for 12 years in the Deep South of America. He later wrote a book about his experiences.
🇩🇪 Dies ist eine wahre Geschichte. Solomon Northup war ein freier Schwarzer Mann, der entführt und 12 Jahre lang in die Sklaverei gezwungen wurde.

Solomon Northup
1829
A Happy Beginning
Dear Diary, I am so happy today! I got married to Anne. We live in New York, and I am a free man. That means I can choose where I work and where I live. I love reading books and playing my violin. When I play, everyone smiles and dances!
1841
The Bad Trick
Dear Diary, something went wrong. I met two men who told me I could play my violin in a circus. I wanted to earn money for my three children, so I went with them. But they were not nice men. They tricked me. I got very sick, and when I woke up, I was in a dark room with chains on my feet. A mean man told me I wasn't Solomon anymore — he called me "Platt" and said I was a slave. I am so sad and scared.
1841
The Long Boat Ride
Dear Diary, I am on a boat called the Orleans. I met a lady named Eliza. She is crying because the bad men took her children away. It makes my heart hurt to see her so sad. I tried to send a secret letter home through a kind sailor, hoping someone will come to save me.
1842
The Scary Forest
Dear Diary, life is very hard now. My new boss, Tibeats, is very mean to me. One day, he tried to hurt me, and I had to run away into a giant swamp. It was very scary. There were alligators and snakes in the water, and dogs were chasing me! I ran and ran until I found a safer place to hide.
1845
Working in the Fields
Dear Diary, I have a new boss named Mr. Epps. Every single day, I have to pick cotton under the hot sun. If I don't pick enough, I get in big trouble. At night, I am so tired, but Mr. Epps makes me play my violin so the others have to dance. I miss my family every single day.
1852
A New Friend
Dear Diary, I met a man named Samuel Bass. He is a builder from Canada. He told me that he thinks slavery is wrong. I took a big chance and told him my real name is Solomon. He promised to help me! He is going to write letters to my friends in New York. Please, let them find me!
1853
Going Home
Dear Diary, a miracle happened! A carriage drove up, and out stepped Henry, a friend from when I was young. He had papers that proved I am a free man. I got to leave that place forever! When I got back to New York, my daughter didn't even know me because I had been gone for 12 years. We all cried and hugged. I am finally home. I am Solomon Northup again.

Step 2: Find the Main Idea Level 2

The main idea is the most important message of the whole text — not every little detail!
(Die Hauptidee ist die wichtigste Aussage des Textes — nicht jedes kleine Detail!)

💡 How to find the main idea: Ask yourself: "Who is this text about, and what is the most important thing that happens to them?"
A main idea is one sentence. It does not include small details like the names of animals, or what someone ate for breakfast!
🇩🇪 Frage dich: „Worum geht es in diesem Text hauptsächlich?" Die Hauptidee ist ein Satz — ohne kleine Details.

a) Which is the Main Idea?

Three sentences describe the text. Which one best describes the overall main idea? Tick the correct one.
(Welcher Satz beschreibt die Hauptidee am besten? Kreuze den richtigen an.)

Step 3: Sort & Summarise Level 3

a) Important or Extra?

A good summary only includes the most important information. Read each sentence and decide:
👍 Important — keep it in your summary  |  ✂️ Extra — leave it out
(Eine gute Zusammenfassung enthält nur die wichtigsten Informationen. Entscheide: Wichtig oder Extra?)

1. Solomon Northup was a free Black man who lived happily in New York with his wife and three children.
2. Solomon loved reading books and playing the violin at parties.
3. In 1841, two men tricked Solomon into coming with them, then sold him into slavery. He woke up in chains.
4. There were alligators and snakes in the swamp, and dogs were chasing Solomon.
5. For many years, Solomon was forced to pick cotton every day and was treated cruelly by his bosses.
6. On the boat, a woman named Eliza was crying because her children had been taken away.
7. Samuel Bass, a builder from Canada, believed slavery was wrong and promised to send letters to New York to help Solomon.
8. In 1853, a friend named Henry arrived with papers proving Solomon was free. After 12 years, Solomon returned home to his family.

You have read Solomon's diary. Now imagine you are Anne — Solomon's wife. You have not heard from Solomon for 12 years.

Write a short diary entry (4–6 sentences) from Anne's point of view on the evening Solomon comes home.
(Stell dir vor, du bist Anne — Solomons Frau. Schreibe 4–6 Sätze aus ihrer Sicht an dem Abend, als Solomon nach Hause kommt.)
